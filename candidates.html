Work with CTTL

Our experienced recruitment team works with qualified teachers, specialist 1:1 tutors, teaching assistants, SEND support staff and other education support professionals.

We focus on short-term contracts, long-term placements and permanent positions across primary, secondary, SEND and alternative provision settings.

Specialist 1:1 tuition

Many of our tuition assignments support learners with EHCPs, complex SEND needs, medical needs or difficulties attending school. Tuition may take place in pupils’ homes, community venues or education settings.

Pay and working arrangements

1:1 tutors are generally paid hourly; teachers and classroom support staff are generally paid daily. PAYE and umbrella company payment options are available, with arrangements discussed before an assignment.

All placements are subject to relevant safeguarding and recruitment checks.
