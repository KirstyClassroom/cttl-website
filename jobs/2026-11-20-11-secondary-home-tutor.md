  ### 1:1 Online Tutor – Greater London


  **Classroom Teachers & Tutors Limited (CTTL)** is looking to hear from
  qualified teachers and experienced tutors interested in providing personalised
  online tuition to KS3/4/5 students.


  We work with schools and local authorities across Greater London, supporting
  learners who may be unable to access traditional education, including pupils
  with EHCPs, SEND, medical needs or anxiety.


  **The Role**


  - Deliver engaging, individualised 1:1 tuition online.

  - Adapt lessons to suit each learner's abilities and educational needs.

  - Support pupils across a range of ages and educational stages.

  - Help learners build confidence, develop their skills and make educational
  progress.


  **What We're Looking For**


  - Qualified teachers, preferably with experience supporting pupils with
  additional needs.

  - Experienced tutors without formal teaching qualifications may also be
  considered, subject to client agreement.

  - Strong communication and organisational skills.

  - A patient, flexible and supportive approach to teaching.

  - A commitment to safeguarding and promoting the welfare of children and young
  people.


  **What We Offer**


  - Opportunities for hourly paid tuition assignments. Generally 15 hours per
  student scheduled at 3hours per day either am or pm. Possible to have 2
  students to make the role full time.

  - PAYE and umbrella company payment options.

  - Support from a friendly, experienced recruitment team.

  - Opportunities to work with learners who benefit from individualised
  education.


  **Register Your Interest**


  To apply for this opportunity, please click the **Apply / Register Interest**
  button below to complete our short online application form. You can also
  upload your CV, although this is optional.


  *This is a general recruitment advert. Specific assignments are subject to
  availability.*
