How we expect to use recruitment information

When you contact us or register as a candidate, you may provide your name, contact details, work preferences, CV, qualifications and employment history. CTTL intends to use relevant information to respond to enquiries, assess suitability and discuss potential recruitment opportunities.

CV handling

CVs may contain personal information. Access should be restricted to authorised recruitment staff, with secure storage, controlled sharing and an appropriate retention and deletion schedule. Any sharing with prospective clients must have a documented lawful basis and follow the final privacy notice.

Your rights

Depending on the circumstances, UK data protection law provides rights including access, rectification, erasure, restriction, objection and data portability. You can also complain to the Information Commissioner's Office (ICO).
