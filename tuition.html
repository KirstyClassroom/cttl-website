Personalised tuition for children and young people from nursery age to 25 across Greater London.

Supporting learners who cannot currently access school

We work with schools and local authorities to arrange 1:1 tuition for children and young people who are unable to access education in a conventional school setting.

Many learners we support have an Education, Health and Care Plan (EHCP), including some with complex special educational needs and disabilities (SEND). Other learners may have medical needs, anxiety affecting attendance or be awaiting a suitable school placement.

Nursery age to 25

Our tuition supports early years, primary, secondary, GCSE, post-16 and young adult learners up to the age of 25.

Where tuition takes place

✓ Pupils’ homes

✓ Suitable community venues

✓ School and education settings

Qualified teachers and experienced tutors

Approximately 99% of our tutors are qualified teachers. We also work with a small number of tutors with extensive individual tuition experience who do not hold formal teaching qualifications. We always discuss this with the referring school or local authority before placing a tutor without a formal teaching qualification.

Safeguarding

All tutors undergo enhanced DBS checks and safeguarding vetting before placement, in line with our procedures following KCSIE 2026 guidance.
